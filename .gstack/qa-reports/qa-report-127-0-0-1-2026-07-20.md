## Issues

### ISSUE-001: Trackers run before consent

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Severity | critical |
| Category | functional / privacy |
| URL | `/` |

Before the visitor makes a choice, Playwright observed `window.posthog` loaded with `optedIn: true`, plus `_fbp`, PostHog, Clarity, and Roistat cookies. Requests were sent to Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics, Meta, PostHog (`g.enso.ro`), Clarity, Bing, and Roistat.

After `Deny`, PostHog correctly changes to opted-out and remains opted-out after reload, but the third-party scripts still load and their existing cookies remain. The active `<GoogleTagManager>` in `app/layout.jsx` is the entry point; the observed behavior indicates the container's individual tags are not all gated by consent.

Evidence: [initial desktop](screenshots/initial.png), [mobile banner](screenshots/mobile-cookie-current.png).

### ISSUE-002: Green Playwright suite does not test the application

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Severity | high |
| Category | functional / test infrastructure |

`tests/example.spec.ts` opens `https://playwright.dev/`. `baseURL` and `webServer` are commented out in `playwright.config.ts`. CI can therefore remain green while ENSŌ LIVING is broken or unavailable.
